Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. About Fix Forward

Fix Forward is a student project prototype showing how campus facility issues can be reported and tracked. It is not an official university service unless formally adopted.

2. Information entered

When you create a report, the app stores its title, category, facility and location, priority, description, optional contact information, optional photo, reporter name, status, assignment, and update history so you can track the issue. Demo profiles may store a name, email address, and selected demo role for the profile and notifications. The sign-in form does not authenticate you; registration only checks the password in the page and does not save or send it. Do not enter real passwords, sensitive personal information, or images of people without permission.

3. Local storage and demo access

Reports, profile details, notifications, notification preferences, and display preferences are stored in this browser using local storage. The app does not send this information to the student developer or university staff, and it does not sync across devices. The campus photograph is bundled with the website; the app does not load it from an image service. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to view or change locally stored data.

Choosing Student, Maintenance staff, or Administrator creates a simulated demo role only. These roles and their screens are not protected by real authentication or server-side authorization; do not use this prototype to grant or protect access to real information.

4. Photos

Optional JPG, PNG, or WebP photos under 1.2 MB are stored in this browser with the report. Browser backups or copies outside this app may remain outside the app's control.

5. Hosting provider

The app has no analytics, advertising, external image service, or application backend. If published online, the hosting provider may collect technical information such as server logs, IP addresses, or browser details under its own policy. Review the selected provider's privacy policy before publishing; this host-side collection is outside this prototype's control.

6. Retention and deletion

Use the administrator demo's Restore sample data control to replace local reports, profiles, and notifications with sample data, or clear this site's browser storage to remove all Fix Forward data, including display preferences. Deletion only affects this browser; there is no central account or backup from which to request deletion.

7. Safety

Do not submit student numbers, contact details, passwords, or other sensitive information. For immediate danger, contact campus staff or emergency services directly rather than relying on this demo.

8. Production readiness

This prototype has no secure backend, real account system, server-side authorization, central storage, or real notifications. A production system requires a reviewed privacy policy, approved data practices, secure authentication, backend-enforced permissions, reliable storage, backups, and verified university contacts.

Privacy questions: Contact the student developer through the course instructor or the official course communication channel. Add a monitored project email address here before public use if one is available.
